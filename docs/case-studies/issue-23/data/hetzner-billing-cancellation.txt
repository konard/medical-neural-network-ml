Billing doc:
- "We have pro-rated billing (you pay AFTER you use our products). Each invoice contains the services for the entire previous month."
- "Billing begins when we give you access to a product. We will use the hourly price as long as the product has not been used
  for almost the entire calendar month. After that, we use the monthly price, which is lower."
- "We exclude from hourly billing products with an annual term, such as domains or SSL certificates; products with a monthly
  term, such as licenses; and products we bill per day or part thereof, such as colocation and add-ons."
- "We round up partial hours. The total cost is never more than the monthly price."
- "If a product still exists on your account, we will invoice you for it, even if you are not actively using it or it is turned off."
- Minimum invoice amount $10 / EUR 10 (charges combined across months until reached).

Cancellation doc (Robot), "Canceling dedicated servers":
- "You can only cancel immediately. We bill by the hour and never exceed the monthly price cap (except for additional hardware and licenses)."
- Data cannot be recovered after cancellation.

GEX product pages: "No minimum contract term", "Cancellation period: immediately"; one-time setup fee applies (GEX45 EUR 209, GEX63 EUR 999, GEX131-1 EUR 1499, excl. VAT).
Server Auction page (https://www.hetzner.com/sb/): "All without setup fees".
